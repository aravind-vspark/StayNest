# Listing copy for operator review

Name: StayNest

Android short description: Find hostels, plan visits and manage your stay with one account.

Apple subtitle: Find a hostel. Feel at home.

## Description

Find your next hostel and keep everyday essentials in one place.
Compare hostel types, prices, amenities and room sharing. Search an area,
save favourites, request a visit and sort hostels by distance when you choose
to share your location.

When you move in, scan the hostel's join QR and request approval. After your
owner assigns a bed, the same account gives you hostel announcements, menus,
maintenance requests, invoices and receipts.

Owners can register properties, manage beds and vacancies, approve tenants,
reply to requests and post updates. Publication requires ownership verification.

Online rent collection is available only for configured properties. Join
requests and enquiries do not reserve a bed. Notification delivery depends
on permissions and service availability. An internet connection is required.
