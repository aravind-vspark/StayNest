Delete your StayNest account

In StayNest, open Account → Delete my account. Confirm your password and type DELETE. You can also start from this website, without the mobile app.

Sign in to delete my account

Your login details, phone, saved places, enquiries, pending joins, authored maintenance requests, sessions and notification subscriptions are removed. Active tenancy is ended. Owned listings are unpublished and their contact phone is removed.

Invoice amounts, payment references, tenancy history and audit records remain linked to an anonymized account. Property business records remain. These records are not automatically deleted by this action. Backups are managed separately by the service operator.

Before public release, the operator must publish their support contact, retention periods and backup deletion schedule in the privacy policy.
