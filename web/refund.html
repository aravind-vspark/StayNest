StayNest Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Effective immediately

This policy outlines the guidelines governing refunds, cancellations, and security deposit returns for bookings and rental transactions made via StayNest.

1. Booking Advance & Move-in Cancellations

  • Cancellation 7+ Days Prior to Check-in: If a tenant cancels a confirmed booking at least 7 calendar days before the agreed move-in date, 100% of the advance booking fee (less applicable banking payment gateway processing charges) is refundable.
  • Cancellation 2 to 6 Days Prior to Check-in: A 50% refund of the advance booking fee will be granted. The remaining 50% is retained by the hostel owner as compensation for holding the vacancy.
  • Cancellation Less Than 48 Hours Before Move-in: Advance booking payments are non-refundable once within 48 hours of scheduled check-in or if the tenant fails to arrive without notice.

2. Monthly Rent Payments

Monthly rent payments processed via StayNest are routed directly to the property owner's registered merchant account to satisfy active tenancy agreements. Rent payments for ongoing occupied periods are strictly non-refundable.

3. Security Deposits & Move-Out Settlements

  • Hostel security deposits collected by owners serve as collateral against unpaid utility bills, property damage, or failure to serve required move-out notice (typically 30 days).
  • Upon serving the agreed vacating notice and conducting an inspection on move-out, the property owner is obligated to refund the net security deposit within 7 to 10 business days.
  • StayNest assists in tracking security deposit amounts and digital deduction summaries via invoice ledgers.

4. Platform Convenience Charges

Platform convenience fees collected on digital checkout cover banking gateway integration, instant SMS notifications, and system infrastructure. These nominal convenience fees are non-refundable once an online transaction has been successfully processed.

5. Duplicate or Failed Transactions

If your account was debited twice due to a network glitch or gateway timeout, the duplicate charge will automatically be reversed by our payment aggregator back to your original payment method (Bank Account, Card, or UPI) within 3 to 5 banking days.

6. Dispute Resolution & Escalation

If you experience an unfair refusal of deposit return or unauthorized deductions, you may submit a dispute ticket to disputes@staynest.in with your invoice receipt and communication history. StayNest will mediate between tenant and host within 48 hours.
