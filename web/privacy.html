StayNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Effective immediately for all StayNest platforms

StayNest ("we", "our", or "platform") is committed to protecting the privacy, security, and digital rights of students, working professionals, and hostel/PG owners using our web and mobile applications. This Privacy Policy outlines how personal data is collected, processed, and safeguarded in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) of India and applicable global data privacy regulations.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Contact Data: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, password hash (encrypted using Argon2id/SHA-256), and user role (Tenant or Property Owner).
  • Property & Tenancy Data: Property listings, hostel address, photos, video tour URLs, room configurations, room assignments, check-in dates, and maintenance tickets.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Monthly rent invoice amounts, payment reference identifiers, transaction status, and owner merchant routing IDs. Note: StayNest never captures or stores credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs. All financial transactions are processed securely via RBI-authorized payment aggregators (e.g., Razorpay/Cashfree).
  • Device & Geolocation Data: Approximate or precise geolocation data is accessed only when you click "Use my location" or "Find nearby PGs". Geolocation calculation occurs locally on your device to rank distance to verified hostels and is never tracked in the background.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Authenticating accounts and validating identity via SMS OTP to prevent fraudulent listings and unauthorized tenant profiles.
  • Facilitating discovery, direct owner enquiries, digital join requests, and room allocations.
  • Generating transparent monthly rent invoices, digital receipts, and settlement routing.
  • Managing hostel maintenance requests and real-time operational notifications.
  • Complying with statutory local lodging registry requirements and crime prevention directives.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Data is shared solely with:

  • Verified Hostel/PG Owners: When you submit an enquiry or join request, your name, verified phone number, and stay duration are shared with the property administrator.
  • Payment Gateways: Tokenized transaction payloads are transmitted securely to authorized payment gateways for rent processing and settlement.
  • SMS / OTP Gateways: Telecommunication aggregators for delivery of one-time authentication passcodes and critical emergency hostel announcements.
  • Law Enforcement Authorities: Disclosed only when legally mandated by a valid court order or statutory Indian legal requirement.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your active personal profile is maintained as long as you maintain an account with StayNest. You can permanently delete your account at any time via Account → Delete my account or via our Account Deletion Portal.

Upon deletion, your personal credentials, phone number, saved properties, and active notification channels are immediately purged. Financial ledgers, invoice totals, and audit records are retained in anonymized format in accordance with Indian tax laws (Income Tax Act & GST Act) for the statutory period (7 years).

5. Security Safeguards

StayNest employs enterprise-grade cryptographic standards, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, Argon2 password hashing, isolated session management with HTTP-only secure cookies, and strict role-based access control (RBAC).
